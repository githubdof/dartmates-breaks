# Dart breaks on Facebook — field notes

Gathered 2026-10-12 from the private Facebook group **Dart Deals and Breaks**
(9.7K members, created Sept 2017, ~75 posts/month, 4 admins) plus related
groups. Source: group posts, comment threads and random.org verification
pages. Names below are hosts/buyers as they appear in public group posts.

## 1. Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Break** (main break) | A group buy of one item. The host splits the price into N **lines**; buyers claim lines; when all are paid, a random draw picks the winner of the item. |
| **Line / spot** | One numbered entry (1..N). A buyer may hold several. "Lines" and "spots" are used interchangeably. |
| **Mini** | A small side break whose prize is **spots in another break** (or in several breaks), run to fill the last unsold lines so the main can close. |
| **Closed** | All lines filled and paid. Host edits the post title to "Closed". |
| **Ran / "CLOSED n RAN"** | Draw has been run. |
| **Setting** | Host comment meaning "I'm setting up the draw now". |
| **Top Spot Wins** | Draw format: entries shuffled, the entry ranked #1 in the final round wins. |
| **Choice** | Winner picks one of several listed items ("Top Spot Wins Choice: X or Y or Z"). |
| **Dressed** | Darts sold complete with flights/shafts. |
| **Random** | A buyer asking the host to assign them N random open lines ("5 random please"). |
| **Bonus** | An extra entry a host adds to the list (seen as "31. Tony O'Neal bonus"). |
| **Free draw / active giveaway** | Host-run loyalty giveaway, free to members who played in their breaks recently. |

## 2. Main break — lifecycle as observed

Example: Jim Roberts, 7 Oct 2026, *Target Phil Taylor Chronos*, 12 lines @ $20
(40 comments). Post body template:

```
Closed                                   <- edited in once filled
Target Phil Taylor Chronos 18 or 20 gram soft 22,24 or 26 gram steel also available
12 lines @20.00 each
No comments on payments                  <- i.e. leave the PayPal/Venmo note blank
Venmo @Jim-Roberts-36
NEW PayPal link
https://www.paypal.me/...
```
plus product photos and screenshots of text (specs, rules).

1. **Post** item, line count, price per line, payment handles. Item may offer
   a choice of weights/tip types; winner chooses after the draw.
2. **Claim**: buyers comment line numbers ("5 please", "6,7", "4,7,11",
   "I'll take the last three", "If 11 is available ill take it") or ask for
   random lines ("5 random please").
3. **Ledger**: host keeps ONE comment with the numbered list and edits it
   as lines are claimed and paid. A ✅ after a name = paid. Unpaid claims
   can be dropped (a buyer replied "9 8" but the final list shows other
   names on 8 and 9).
4. **Nudge**: "Only three spots left please send payment if you haven't".
5. **Close**: title edited to "Closed" once all lines are ✅.
6. **Draw**: host comments "Setting", runs a random.org *Multi-Round
   Giveaway* with the line list as entries, posts the verify link
   (`giveaways.random.org/verify/<code>`).
7. **Announce**: "Congratulations …", winner picks their variant, host ships.
   Advent-calendar break said "Ships by December 1st" (pre-order).

Observed sizes: 10 lines @ $10, 11 @ $8, 12 @ $20, 13 @ $20, 18 @ $10,
18 @ $4 (mini). Lines × price ≈ retail value (advent calendar: "Over $238
value", 18 × $10 = $180).

## 3. Minis — lifecycle as observed

### 3a. Single-parent mini ("Taylor mini")
Parent: Chronos break, 13 lines @ $20, stuck with 4 unsold lines ($80).
```
Closed
Mini to close
1st gets 2 spots
2nd gets last 2 spots
10 lines @$8.00 each          <- 10 × $8 = $80 = exactly the 4 open spots
```
- Claims: "6,7", "3,5", "4,8", "I'll take lat 4" (last four).
- Ledger comment with ✅ per paid line; Jimmy had 4 lines, others 2 each.
- Host: "Setting" → random.org giveaway titled **"Taylor mini"**, 10
  entries, **13 rounds** (round counts follow no rule — confirmed by the
  user), final round ranking is the result. 1st = line 10 (Jimmy), 2nd = line 7 (Shane).
- Host: "Congratulations to the top 2 head over to the main and pick your
  spots in order" → winners choose which open line numbers they take in
  the parent, in rank order.

**General mini rules extracted**
- prize ladder: ordered list of (rank → n spots in parent P); ladder sums
  to the number of open parent spots.
- lines × price ≈ open spots × parent price (hosts round up a bit).
- random.org final-round ranking decides ranks 1..k.
- winners then choose their line numbers in the parent, in rank order
  ("head over to the main(s) and pick your spots in order").
- optional: priority window for parent participants, "no randoms",
  straight fill, serial minis (#1, #2 …) for a big parent.

Minis can be posted while the parent is still open (3b) or after it has
stalled (3a). The parent closes only when every line is paid, whether by a
direct buyer or by a mini winner.

## 4. Draw mechanics
- Tool: random.org **Multi-Round Giveaway Service**. Entries = the ledger
  (one entry per line, name of owner). Rounds vary per draw (6, 7, 9, 10,
  12, 13 seen). Each round fully shuffles; the final round's order is the
  result. Rank 1 wins (Top Spot Wins); ranks 2, 3 matter for minis with
  several prizes.
- Hosts post the verify URL; anyone can check code, entry list, timestamps.
- Because the only proof is the host's link, a host can run the draw twice
  on two accounts and post the favourable one (see §6).
- **Proof expires**: random.org deletes giveaways one month after they
  run ("Giveaways expire 1 month after they have been completed"). The
  10 Sept 2026 draw `owqsdn` already returns "There is no giveaway that
  matches this verification code" on 12 Oct. The 12 Oct draw `judeyi`
  still resolves. So there is no durable record of who won what.

## 5. Payments & trust today (we will NOT handle money)
- PayPal.me links and Venmo handles in the post. "No comments on
  payments" / "Friends and family only no comments" — hosts want F&F (no
  fees, no buyer protection, no note that says what it's for).
- 2021 PSA from an admin: "Turn off Facebook pay!! Scammers are attacking
  this community and stealing money through FBpay".
- Payment confirmation is manual: buyer replies "Sent", host adds ✅.
- Trust = reputation of the host inside the group + admin badge. No
  ratings, no history, no delivery confirmation anywhere.

## 6. Documented scams / failure modes
- **Rigged draw** (Sept 2021, "R&R Darts Break Room"): host ran the same
  break's draw on two random.org accounts; posted the one where a friend
  won; defended it as "a mistake". Members: "Same thing happened to me …
  in one of her breaks a while back."
- **Named scammer posts**: "Do not do business with this guy! He is a
  scammer. Who was all scammed by this guy?" — warnings travel as posts,
  easily lost.
- **FB Pay account takeovers** (2021).
- **Unpaid claims** blocking a break from closing → the reason minis exist.
- Hosts sometimes **turn comments off** after closing, so the ledger and
  result can't be discussed.

## 7. Pain points of doing this on Facebook (platform opportunities)
1. Line ledger is a hand-edited comment; collisions and typos happen.
2. Break details (lines, price, rules, results) are often **screenshots**,
   not text → not searchable, not accessible.
3. No link between a mini and its parent(s) except the host's memory.
4. No view of "what's open right now", "which spots are free", "what do I
   hold", "what did I win", "has it shipped".
5. No host reputation beyond vibes; scam warnings are scattered posts.
6. Draw proof is an external link the host controls.
7. Members get no notification when a spot they wanted frees up, when a
   break closes, or when they win.
8. Loyalty programs (free draws for active members) are run by hand.

## 7b. Break For Darts group (11.7K members, 2017–2026, paused by admins May 2026)
Run by **Break for Darts LLC** (breakfordarts.com, now dead). Bigger scale
and more formalised than Dart Deals and Breaks. Vocabulary & rules seen:

- **Straight fill**: lines are handed out in order as people claim a
  *count* ("leave # of spots"), nobody picks numbers. Opposite of
  "pick your own spots". "No Randoms" = you must pick, no random assignment.
- **Priority window**: "open only to those with spots in the main for the
  first hour" — main-break participants get first dibs on a mini.
- **Numbered breaks**: hosts give each break an ID ("WB1762 Mini",
  "WB1800 - MINI", "WB1744-1763" for a batch video draw).
- **Serial minis for a huge main**: "The ultimate Elysian break!! All 3
  sets!! 1 winner!! … 240 spots @20, top spot will be the winner. paypal
  only" ($4,800). Then "Mini #1 … 15 total spots up for grabs … 100 lines
  @ $3 straight fill … 1st gets 6 spots…"; "Mini #3 … 100 spots at $4 …
  20 spots in the main up for grabs … 1st-7, 2nd-6, 3rd-4, 4th-3"; Mini #4
  same shape. So a mini can have a 4+ rank payout ladder and a main can be
  filled by several minis over days.
- **Multi-prize main**: "Elysian steel tip gen 5 break … Winner to receive
  steel tip Elysians, 2nd to receive winmau steel tip board, 3rd place to
  receive target Alan Norris darts … 115 lines at 10 dollars".
- **Mini with few expensive lines**: "ELYSIAN MINI WB1800 — 5 Spots at
  $20.00 - No Randoms — Top Spot receives 2 spots in the Elysian choice
  break".
- **Free-spot bonus**: "9 PAID spots up for grabs - pick your own spots,
  get more spots if you find the free ones!" (hidden free lines as a game).
- **Wheels**: another host runs "wheels" (og / economy / baller) — spin-
  the-wheel style prize draws at different price points.
- **Video draws**: "Video for the WB1744-1763 will run tomorrow around
  5 pm cst" — batch of breaks drawn live on video.
- **Admin rules**: "No comments with payments on PayPal! … If they make you
  do a comment like for Venmo, just write 'Stuff' or 'Food'";
  "you need to actively participate in the community buying lines to be
  allowed to list your items"; "You must be active to post"; "NO PRICE
  GOUGING"; LLC keeps an exclusivity list of items only it may break.
- **Disputes**: "YES I did a paypal charge back against [host] … sent the
  wrong jersey … did not respond to my messages" (buyer warning, comments
  turned off); "Almost everyone in my last break failed to send their
  payments to friends and family" (host complaint); "Closed and refunded"
  (host cancelled a break and refunded); admin warning against Gran Board
  3 pre-order breaks because the maker offered no pre-orders.
- Hosts' "Closed no interest → Adjusted to straight fill, leave # of
  spots, let's spark some interest" — breaks get re-priced/re-formatted
  mid-flight when they stall.

## 9. What this means for the platform (no money handled)

Constraints given: we never touch payments; trust comes from a feedback
system. So the product is a **ledger + draw + reputation** layer that sits
beside whatever PayPal/Venmo flow hosts already use.

Core objects (from the observations above):
- **Host** (user who can list), **Member** (user who claims lines).
- **Break**: item(s) (possibly a *choice* list, possibly upgraded later),
  `lines`, `price_per_line`, fill mode (`pick` | `straight` | `random`
  allowed?), priority window, status (`open` → `filled` → `drawn` →
  `fulfilled`), draw record, optional parent links.
- **Line**: number, owner (member), `claimed_at`, `paid` flag set by host,
  source (`direct` | `mini:<id>`), optional `bonus`/`free` flag.
- **Mini**: a Break whose prizes are `(rank → n spots in Break P)` rather
  than an item; may target several parents; parent spots are reserved for
  the mini while it runs and released/assigned after the draw.
- **Draw**: entries = lines in order, rounds, seed/verification, final
  ranking; winners for ranks 1..k; "winner picks spot" step for minis.
- **Fulfilment**: winner's variant choice, shipped / received marks.
- **Feedback**: per completed break, both directions (buyer → host:
  paid-and-received-as-described; host → buyer: paid on time). Public
  host stats: breaks run, on-time ship %, disputes, repeat buyers.

Features that map 1:1 to today's pain:
1. Click-to-claim spots with live availability (no edited-comment ledger).
2. Host marks "paid" per line; buyers see their own lines and status.
3. Built-in verifiable draw, our own (decided 2026-10-12, no random.org):
   frozen hashed entry snapshot → seed the host can't choose
   (commit–reveal or server CSPRNG) → deterministic seeded Fisher–Yates →
   permanent public draw page with full ranking. Exactly one draw per
   break. Ranking serves both mains (rank 1) and minis (ranks 1..k).
4. Mini creation from an open break: pick open spots → auto-build ladder
   and line price; winners pick parent spots inside the app.
5. Notifications: spot freed, break filled, you won, item shipped.
6. Host reputation + scam/dispute flags that don't get lost in the feed.
7. Loyalty tooling: "active member" lists, free draws, priority windows.
8. Structured break posts (searchable), with optional image gallery.
