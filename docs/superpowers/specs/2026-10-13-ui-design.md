# Breaks UI design — direction and system

Status: panel-revised draft, awaiting owner approval. Companion to the
platform spec (`2026-10-13-dart-breaks-platform-design.md`, §13 pages).
This document fixes the visual identity, the design tokens, the layout of
every v1 screen, and the rules the implementation plans argue from.

Panel (three reviewers: product and flow, mobile interaction and
accessibility, frontend architecture) reviewed the first draft on
2026-10-13. Their must-change items are folded in below; their reports
are summarised in §10.

## 1. Subject, audience, job

**Subject.** Group buys of darts gear. The physical world is the
dartboard: sisal board, black and white segments, red and green rings,
the wire "spider", the numbers ring. The community's words are *break*,
*line* / *spot*, *closed*, *ran*, *top spot wins*, *straight fill*,
*random*, *take the rest*.

**Audience.** Hobbyists in Facebook groups, almost all on phones,
arriving from a link the host posted, usually inside Facebook's in-app
browser with no session. Many never see the landing page. Hosts run
several breaks a week and live in the payment ledger, often with Venmo
or PayPal open in another app.

**Primary job of each surface.**

| Surface | Job in one sentence |
|---|---|
| Break page | Show at a glance how many lines are left, let me claim and pay in under a minute, and show me the host ticked me off. |
| Host ledger | Match the payment I just got to a person, tick them off, chase the late ones, run the draw when closed. |
| Result page | Prove the draw was fair, in a form that is fun to share. |
| Feed | Find a break worth joining. |
| Host profile | Decide whether to trust this host with my money. |

**Design constraints from the owner.** Breaks run up to **50 lines**
(`MAX_LINES`, one constant in the domain). Every layout is designed at
50 first and must still read at 6. Bonus lines (numbers above N, at most
max(1, 10% of N) as the platform spec rules) make the grid at most 55 cells.

## 2. Direction: the board

One memorable element: **the grid is a dartboard numbers ring laid
flat.** Cells are square, touch each other along shared hairline wires,
carry their number in a condensed face, and change state the way board
segments do: white (open), black with a white number (paid). As a break
closes, the board goes from white to black. Everything around the grid is
quiet paper and ink so the grid carries the page, inside the app and in
the link preview Facebook draws.

What we are not doing: cream paper with a serif and clay accent; dark
mode by default; identical rounded cards with the same shadow; eyebrows
in caps; middle-dot meta strings; arrows on buttons; entrance animations.

## 3. Tokens

> **Superseded in part by Mosaic** (`2026-10-14-mosaic-ui-design.md`, §1.1 and §4). Paper tokens keep their values
> and their uses on paper surfaces. The board has new tokens with new names (`--board-*`, `--lamp-0` … `--lamp-4`,
> `--kit-dark`, `--kit-light`). On the board, mine is the inner ring, green marks only the 2nd and 3rd place rings,
> red marks the top-spot ring and the host-view overdue edge, and flight faces are never red or green. Wall routes
> add Big Shoulders 800 (the Display cut, pinned) for titles, the caller banner, the checkout count and the result display. Motion
> below is replaced in full by Mosaic §7. "No caps labels" stands.

### Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `paper` | `#F6F5F1` | Page background. Slightly warm grey, like sisal. Keep it at this value and no warmer. |
| `card` | `#FFFFFF` | Surfaces on paper: the grid, the ledger, sheets. |
| `ink` | `#000000` | Text, paid cells, primary buttons. Pure black, as on a board. |
| `ink-2` | `#5C5A55` | Secondary text. |
| `wire-strong` | `#86827A` | Every edge that carries meaning: cell borders, input borders, secondary button outlines. 3.8:1 on card. |
| `wire` | `#C9C6BE` | Decorative dividers and table rules only. |
| `wire-2` | `#E6E4DE` | Subtle fills: table zebra, disabled surfaces. |
| `green` | `#1E7A4C` | Mine, and paid confirmed by the host. Board green. |
| `red` | `#C8102E` | Overdue, errors, destructive confirmations. Board red. |

Rules. Red is never a button background; it is a state. Green means
exactly two things: *my* lines and money the host has *confirmed*. Sent
(the buyer's claim) is never green. Primary actions are ink on paper
(black button, white text); secondary actions are outlined in
`wire-strong`; text actions are ink underlined. Colour never carries a
state alone: every state also has a shape (§4). Tokens are CSS variables
mapped through Tailwind v4's `@theme inline`, so the later dark mode is a
variable remap, not a redesign.

Measured contrast: ink on paper 19.3:1; ink-2 on paper 6.3:1 and on card
6.9:1; card on green 5.3:1; card on red 5.9:1; green on paper 4.9:1;
wire-strong on card 3.8:1, on paper 3.5:1. Rule: every text pair at
least 4.5:1, every state-bearing edge or fill difference at least 3:1.

### Type

One family, two widths: **Barlow** for text and **Barlow Condensed** for
numbers and display, via `next/font/google` (fallback: vendored files
through `next/font/local` if the build cannot reach Google). Weights
400, 500, 600 and condensed 600 only. Tabular numerals wherever money or
counts appear.

| Role | Face | Size / line | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display (result winner, landing) | Barlow Condensed | 34 / 36 | 600 |
| Break title | Barlow Condensed | 26 / 28, max two lines | 600 |
| Grid numbers | Barlow Condensed | 18 / 1, never below 16 | 600 |
| Headline (section) | Barlow | 20 / 26 | 600 |
| Body | Barlow | 16 / 24 | 400 |
| Small (meta, table) | Barlow | 14 / 20 | 400 |
| Micro (legend, badges) | Barlow | 12 / 16 | 500 |

Sentence case everywhere. No caps labels, except quoted community idiom
in share text ("CLOSED n RAN"). Line length under 70 characters.

### Space and shape

4-point scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48. Page gutter 16 on phones, 24
above 640. Corner radius: 4 on inputs, 8 on sheets and cards, 999 on
pills; cells are square with no radius. One shadow only, on bottom
sheets: `0 -8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,.12)`. Cards on paper use a `wire`
hairline, not a shadow.

### Motion

Two moments only. (1) A cell changing state: 120 ms fill, cosmetic; the
final state is set immediately. (2) The result reveal: the shuffled
order deals out over about 1.5 s, then the top spot's card lifts and
turns green. The result is in the DOM before the animation and the deal
layer is `aria-hidden`; a **Skip** control shows during the deal; the
deal plays once per viewer and later visits offer **Replay the draw**.
Both honour `prefers-reduced-motion` (instant, at most a 150 ms fade).
No animation library.

## 4. The grid

> **Superseded in part by Mosaic** (§1.1 and §5). On wall boards `--board-steel` is the wire; `columnsFor` and the
> cell sizes are unchanged. The states table is replaced row by row by Mosaic §5: paid is the holder's colour, the
> dashed sent edge is retired, the two-tone ring replaces the corner flag, brackets replace the inset and the check,
> overdue keeps the red edge and "!" on the Mosaic face, reserved keeps the double rule and "m", and the bonus ink
> face and "+" are retired (black now means won). The struck line is the sniped state, drawn in ink. The legend is
> drawn as mini cells, the group label is "Lines, 12 to go", and the board's focus ring is ink and cream.

### Columns and cells

Cells touch. Each cell is one square tile whose whole area is the tap
target; cells share 1 px `wire-strong` borders so the grid reads as the
spider wire. No CSS gap. Column count comes from the grid's own width
(container query) and the line count:

| Lines | Max columns |
|---|---|
| 2–12 | 4 |
| 13–30 | 6 |
| 31–55 | 8 |

`cols = min(maxColumns, floor(width / 44))`, so at 50 lines: 8 columns
at 352 pt and up (390-pt phones, 44.75 pt cells, 7 rows, 313 pt tall);
7 columns at 308 pt and up (360-pt Androids, 46.9 pt cells, 8 rows,
375 pt); 6 columns at 264 pt and up (320-pt phones, 48 pt cells, 9 rows,
432 pt). Gutters never shrink below 16 to rescue a column. Last rows are
left-aligned, never centred. Above 640 the 600-wide column takes 10
columns of 56 pt for 31 to 55 lines, so rows of ten map to the numbers.
Bonus cells sit after line N on a new row, separated by a 12 pt gap and
a "Bonus" micro label.

`touch-action: manipulation` on the grid so a quick double tap toggles
twice instead of zooming. Fixed cell sizes so the font swap cannot move
a cell under a finger.

### States, shape first

| State | Face | Border | Number | Mark |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open | card | wire-strong 1 px | ink | none |
| Taken (claimed, unpaid) | card with a 45° hatch (ink-2 at 35%, 1 px every 4 pt) | wire-strong 1 px | ink-2 on a small card chip | none |
| Sent | hatch as taken | ink 2 px dashed | ink on chip | none |
| Paid | ink | ink | card | none |
| Mine (on any state) | as state | as state | as state (a paid cell keeps its white number: green on black is 3.9:1) | 10 pt corner flag top-right: green on light faces, white on the ink face; "yours" in the accessible name |
| Selected (about to claim) | card | ink 3 px inset | ink, weight 600 (700 is not loaded) | check glyph in the flag position |
| Overdue (host and admin only) | hatch as taken | red 2 px | ink on chip | "!" glyph bottom-right |
| Reserved for a mini | card | wire-strong double rule | ink-2 | "m" micro label |
| Bonus (number > N) | ink | ink | card | "+" in the flag position |

The server sends `overdue` as a flag only to the host or an admin; every
other viewer gets `taken`. Selection is client-only. Fills therefore
mean only open, taken, paid; green and red reinforce, they never carry.

Legend under the grid: five swatches with words, no icons: open, taken,
sent, paid, yours. When the viewer holds lines, one line of text follows:
"You have 4, 11 and 27." Under that, one quiet line: "Draw locked before
opening. How it works", linking to the explainer.

### Grid and list

A segmented control above the grid: **Grid | List**, Grid default. List
shows one row per line (number in condensed, who holds it, state word);
two columns above 640, one on phones. Who holds a line is shown only to
the host and to participants, as the server already enforces; anonymous
viewers see "taken". In Grid, tapping a taken cell shows a small popover,
"Line 7: @mike, paid" (or "Line 7: taken"). The list is the screenshot
surface: it carries the break title and short link at the top and fits
one phone screenshot up to 25 lines.

### Accessibility

The grid is a `role="group"` labelled "Lines, 19 of 50 open". Each cell
is a native `<button>`: open cells use `aria-pressed` for selection;
taken cells are `aria-disabled` but focusable, named "Line 12, taken",
"Line 12, paid", "Line 9, yours, sent". Buyer-side names never include
another buyer's handle. A skip link before the grid jumps to the claim
bar. Arrow keys move focus within the group. The bar total is a polite
live region ("3 lines selected, $15"). `scroll-padding-bottom` equals
the bar height so a focused cell is never under the bar.

### Selection and claiming

Tap toggles selection. The bar names the lines, not just a count:
"Lines 7, 12, 31, $15" (past the width: "7, 12, 31 +2"). When the
selection reaches the per-person limit, remaining open cells become
`aria-disabled` and a note reads "You can hold 5 lines in this break";
tapping a disabled cell shows the note. If a refresh shows a selected
line taken by someone else, it is struck in the grid, dropped from the
bar, and announced: "Line 7 was just taken." The page refreshes itself
every 20 s while visible and open, and on returning to the tab.

**Claim commits immediately**, before any payment step, so lines are not
sniped while the buyer is in Venmo. Partial success is normal: the sheet
opens on the outcome, "You have lines 4 and 11. Line 7 was just taken."
with one inline action, **Take a random open line instead**.

When nothing is selected the bar offers the community's other habits:
**Pick for me** with a − 1 + stepper (bounded by open lines, the
per-person limit and the global unpaid cap) and **Claim 1 random**, the
count following the stepper. When 5 or fewer lines are open, **Take the
last 3** appears. Picking mode is a host setting with three values:
*Pick or random* (default), *Pick only, no randoms*, *Straight fill*. On
straight fill the grid is read-only and the bar reads "Straight fill.
Say how many; you get the next open numbers."

Signed out, the bar says **Sign in to claim 3 lines**. The selection
survives sign-in in the URL (`?pick=4,7,11`); after onboarding the user
lands back on the confirm step with the lines held.

## 5. Screens

> **Superseded in part by Mosaic** (§1.1 and §8). Landing hero: the kit board of the most recent ran break with its
> black bull cells. Feed: chips "Newest" and "Closest to full", cards on the wall with "12 to go" and no fill bar.
> Break page: the media row "50 lines, $5 a line", "Hosted by @jim" with the kit swatch, "Pay within 24 hours." in
> the claim sheet, the status pill beside the checkout count, the Mosaic §8 pixel budget. Ledger: unchanged, and its
> header gains the host record. Result: "Top spot: line 7" in display type, the board in the viewer's view with the
> black bulls, and the Mosaic §7 reveal. `/me`: your own lines in your flight, and a plain history. Host profile:
> the host record and the kit.

Phone first; widths quoted at 390. Above 640 the same column sits
centred at 600 wide; the host ledger alone widens to 960 and becomes a
table.

### 5.1 Shell

Header, 56 pt: wordmark left (**Breaks**, Barlow Condensed 600, 22 pt,
ink), then on the right either **Sign in** or the user's avatar initial.
Three destinations in a bottom tab bar on phones: **Browse**,
**My lines**, **Host** (Host shows only to hosts and admins). Above 640
they move into the header. The tab bar is hidden on `/b/{id}` and inside
any sheet, so there is never more than one bar at the bottom. Bars and
sheets pad with `max(12px, env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`; the viewport
meta sets `viewport-fit=cover`; sheets use `dvh` units, never `100vh`.
The sticky bar hides while a text input is focused. Footer: terms, how
draws work, contact; one small line.

### 5.2 Landing `/`

Hero is a real break: the most recent *ran, not voided* break, its grid
rendered small and fully black with the one green winning cell, linking
to its result, captioned "Ran 2 hours ago. Anyone can check the draw."
(or "Ran on Tuesday" past 48 h). One sentence: "Group buys for darts
gear. See every host's record, and check every draw yourself." Two
buttons: **Browse breaks** (primary), **How draws work** (secondary).
Then the feed's first six cards and one quiet line for hosts: "Hosting
on Facebook now? Breaks is invite-only for hosts. Ask for an invite."

### 5.3 Feed `/breaks`

Sort chips (Newest, Filling fast, Almost full) and a host filter. Cards
are horizontal: 96-pt square thumbnail left (the break's mini grid
until photos exist); title; "19 of 50 left" with the two-tone fill bar
(paid in ink, taken in hatch); "$5 a line"; host with the trust line.
Trust line: "212 breaks, 98% would buy again" once the reputation plan
lands; until then "12 breaks run, since Oct 2026", or "New host" under
five. Empty state: "No open breaks right now. Hosts post most evenings."

### 5.4 Break page `/b/{id}`

Pixel budget at 390 wide, 50 lines, so the whole grid sits above the
fold even in Safari's 664-pt viewport:

| Block | Height | Running |
|---|---|---|
| Header | 56 | 56 |
| Media row: 88-pt square thumbnail left (tap opens the photo; the break's mini grid when there is no photo); right: title (26/28, two lines) and "19 of 50 left" in condensed 20 with "$5 a line" beside it | 12 + 88 | 156 |
| Small line: "Pay within 24 hours." then host handle and trust line, the whole line a 44-pt link | 8 + 20 | 184 |
| Grid, 7 rows | 12 + 313 | 509 |
| Legend | 8 + 16 | 533 |
| Sticky bar + safe area | 64 + 34 | 631 |

At 360 wide the grid is 8 rows and the legend sits at the fold. At 320
the page scrolls; the bar keeps the selection visible. The status pill
(Open, Closed, Running draw, Ran, Cancelled) sits beside the count. The
full photo and any further photos live in the details fold.

Below the grid: **Share** (copies state-specific text, §6) and, for the
host, **Host tools**, both page-level buttons under the legend; then the
details fold: description, prizes (top spot gets X, or a choice; 2nd
place if any), lines per person, picking mode, what happens when it
closes, the host's payment handles with copy buttons, "Questions? Ask
Jim" (a slot for the thread, later).

**The sticky bar** has one job at a time:

| Viewer state | Left | Right |
|---|---|---|
| Signed out, selection | "Lines 7, 12, $10" | **Sign in to claim 2 lines** |
| Signed in, selection | "Lines 7, 12, $10" | **Claim 2 lines** |
| Signed in, nothing selected | **Pick for me** − 1 + | **Claim 1 random** (and **Take the last 3** when 5 or fewer open) |
| Holds unpaid lines not marked Sent | "You owe $10 to @Jim-Roberts-36" (tap reopens the pay block) | **I've sent it** |
| Just marked Sent | "Sent. Jim will tick you off when it arrives." | claim controls return |
| Closed | "Closed. Every line is paid. Waiting for Jim to run the draw." | |
| Closed 3+ days, not drawn | "Closed 3 days ago, not drawn yet." | |
| Running draw | "Jim started the draw. The result is in about a minute." | |
| Ran | | **See the result** |
| Cancelled | "Cancelled by Jim. Your refund comes from Jim, not Breaks." | **Refund received** (if the viewer paid) |

**The claim sheet** opens after the claim commits. Top: the outcome and
the held lines as mini cells. Then the pay block: "Send $10 to
@Jim-Roberts-36", **Copy handle** first, then **Pay with Venmo** and
**Pay with PayPal** as full-width secondary buttons (deep link, falling
back to the web profile where the scheme is blocked in the in-app
browser), "Leave the note blank.", then "Draw locked before you joined"
with the short commitment. Then **I've sent it** (primary) with the
one-time hint "No need to comment Sent on Facebook.", and **I'll pay
later** (text) with the absolute deadline above it: "Pay by 9:40 pm
tomorrow or Jim can release your lines." A **Give back these lines**
text action withdraws unpaid lines.

### 5.5 Host ledger (in the break, and `/host`)

A full-screen sheet over the break page. Header: "19 of 50 paid" with
the two-tone fill bar; a search field "Name, handle or amount" that
matches display name, handle, payment name and owed amount; filter
chips All, Sent, Unpaid, Overdue.

Rows are grouped by buyer, at least 56 pt tall:

- Line 1: **display name** bold, `@handle` in ink-2, payment name if the
  buyer gave one ("pays as John Smith"), amount owed right-aligned in
  condensed tabular figures.
- Line 2: their lines as mini cells (each its own tap target for
  per-line marking in the expanded row), state word, and on overdue rows
  "4 h late" in red.
- Micro line under the handle: "First break with you" or "2 late
  before" (from the reputation plan; omitted until then).
- Right edge: one **Paid** button, 44 pt tall, marking all their unpaid
  lines.

Order is fixed when the ledger opens (Sent first, then unpaid by claim
time, then overdue, then paid at the bottom) and rows **never move under
the thumb**: marking Paid changes the row in place to "Paid" in green
with **Undo** beside it for 8 s and a polite snackbar "Marked @dave paid
(3 lines, $15)." Rows re-sort only when the host changes the filter or
reopens the ledger. Un-marking later uses the row menu and is logged.
**Release** lives in the expanded row, never beside Paid, and confirms
with the consequence: "Release lines 4 and 11 from @mike? They reopen
now and Mike is told." with **Release 2 lines** and **Keep**. The row
menu also offers **Add a bonus line** with the allowance shown ("2 of 5
bonus lines left"). A **Remind** action (DM: "Jim is waiting on $10 for
lines 4 and 11. Pay by 9:40 pm or they go back on the board.") and
**Remind all** on the Overdue filter arrive with the messaging plan.

Bottom action: **Run draw** once closed; **Mark shipped** after the
draw (with the reputation plan).

`/host` lists the host's breaks grouped by state (Open, Closed, Running
draw, Ran, Drafts) with the mini fill bar, **New break** at the top, and
above the list a strip "7 sent, waiting for you, across 3 breaks" that
opens a merged ledger with the break title on each row.

### 5.6 New break `/host/new`

One screen, grouped fields, defaults from the host's last break. Title.
Prize group: "Top spot gets: [item]" with **Add a choice** ("or ...")
and **Add 2nd place**. Lines (stepper, 2–50), price per line, hours to
pay (24), lines per person, picking mode (three values, §4), payment
handles, payment instructions, description. No photo field until the
storage plan. Preview strip at the bottom: "50 lines at $5 = $250" and a
tiny rendering of the grid shape. **Save draft** and **Open break**.
Drafts can be edited; they are not viewable by anyone but the host.

### 5.7 Result `/b/{id}/result`

Server-rendered final state first: "Top spot: line 7" in display type
above the winner's handle, the winning line as one large green cell,
and the full order. JavaScript then plays the deal once (§3 motion).
Then "Checked. Locked before opening, mixed with public randomness." and
a **Check it yourself** fold that runs the browser verifier and shows
the commitment, the beacon round and the seed, each in a copy field.
**Share** builds the "CLOSED n RAN" text. The fake-beacon warning, when
present, is a red hairline box at the top.

### 5.8 My lines `/me`

Grouped by break, most recent first: title, my cells, state word (open,
sent, paid, won, shipped), amount owed if any, **How to pay Jim** (reopens
the pay block), **Give back unpaid lines** with a confirm naming the
lines, and after a win "Won. Tell Jim your choice" for choice breaks,
then **Got it** when shipped. Past breaks collapse under "Earlier".

### 5.9 Host profile `/h/{handle}`

Handle, since-when, the trust line explained in one sentence ("98% of
winners would buy again, 212 breaks run"; interim: "12 breaks run since
Oct 2026"), median days to ship when available, then the host's open
breaks as feed cards and a count of past ones.

### 5.10 Sign-in and onboarding

Inside Facebook's in-app browser the emailed link opens another browser
and loses the session, so sign-in is **code first, in the same tab**:
"We emailed you a 6-digit code. Type it here." with the link as the
fallback. A buyer arriving from a claim gets one onboarding screen:
handle (prefilled from the email, editable, live availability), the
18+ and terms checkboxes, **Continue to claim**; display name defaults
to the handle and is editable in `/account`. Direct sign-ups get the
same screen with display name shown. Copy: "Pick a handle. Hosts and
buyers see this name."

## 6. Writing

> **Superseded in part by Mosaic** (§1.1, §3 and §9). The count everywhere is "N to go" ("12 to go, $5 a line" in
> the preview caption) and the feed sort is "Closest to full"; "19 of 50 left", "Filling fast", "Almost full" and
> "You hold …" are retired. Mosaic §3 adds the banned words (the heat words, streak, level, "lucky colour",
> winnings, "You won", caps chips, betting words near a kit), pinned by `src/lib/copy-audit.test.ts`.

Buttons say what happens: Claim 3 lines, I've sent it, Mark paid,
Release 2 lines, Run draw, Open break, Share. The same verb follows
through: Claim → "Claimed", Mark paid → "Paid". State words are the
community's: Open, Closed (every line paid), Running draw, Ran,
Cancelled. Feed sort "Almost full", not "Closing soon". Errors say what
went wrong and what to do: "Line 7 was just taken. Pick another." Empty
states point at the next action. No exclamation marks. No middle dots:
"3 lines, $15"; "$5 a line. Pay within 24 hours."; "212 breaks, 98%
would buy again". Money is "$5", "$5.50", "$1,250", never "$5.00/line".
Never raffle, lottery, odds, bet, ticket, jackpot, lucky.

Share text by state (copied to the clipboard; link previews carry a
server-rendered image of the thumbnail beside the grid in its current
state with "19 of 50 left, $5 a line"):

- open: "Taylor Chronos break. 50 lines at $5. Pick yours: <link>"
- nudge (5 or fewer open): "Only 3 spots left. If you've claimed, please send payment. <link>"
- closed: "CLOSED. Taylor Chronos. Draw tonight. <link>"
- ran: "CLOSED n RAN. Top spot: line 7, @mike. Check the draw: <link>"

## 7. Accessibility and quality floor

> **Superseded in part by Mosaic** (§1.1, §5 and §11). On the board the focus ring is a 2 px `--board-ink` outline
> with a 2 px cream ring outside it; on paper the 2 px green ring stands. The 44 pt target holds (cells 44.75 px at
> 390). Axe runs over the member view, the kit view, the result page, `/account` and the host's break view with an
> overdue line; flight contrast is pinned by `flights.test.ts`.

Tap targets 44 pt (the full cell, the full row button). Visible focus
ring, 2 px green at 2 px offset, everywhere. No state by colour alone
(§4). Contrast as measured in §3. Reduced motion honoured. Sheets are
native `<dialog>` elements: focus trapped, Escape closes, focus returns
to the opener, body scroll locked. Reading a break and its result works
without JavaScript, including the winner; claiming needs it. Relative
times and overdue flags are computed on the server and passed as
strings so hydration never disagrees.

## 8. What waits for its backend

Each of these is drawn here with a fallback and arrives with its own
plan: photos (storage plan; fallback is the mini grid thumbnail); the
trust percentage, late-payment history and median ship days
(reputation plan; fallback "N breaks run, since …" or "New host");
Remind and the thread slot (messaging plan); Mark shipped and Got it
(reputation plan); Cancelled transitions (needs a cancel command;
display-only until then); reserved-for-mini cells (minis plan;
render-only); dark mode; the contact address.

## 9. Implementation notes for the planner

Tailwind v4, CSS-first: `src/app/tokens.css` with raw variables and
`@theme inline`; `globals.css` replaced; no `tailwind.config`. Fonts in
`src/app/fonts.ts`. Components in `src/components/`: Cell, Grid,
MiniCells, Legend, FillBar, StatusPill, Avatar, HostLine, Button,
CopyButton, CopyField, Stepper, Sheet, StickyBar, ClaimBar, PaySheet,
BreakInteractive, LedgerRow, LedgerSheet, FilterChips, BreakCard,
Banner, Toast, AppHeader, TabBar, Footer, HandleField, Reveal,
GridPreview. Grid and Cell are presentational and server-safe; one
client island per break page owns selection, `useOptimistic` over
server props (never a copied `lines` state), the taken-line recovery and
the auto-refresh. Action failures carry typed details (`taken`,
`limit`). Grid shaping moves into a pure, tested function with the
host-only overdue rule. Domain additions first: `claim_mode`, per-buyer
ledger query, single-transaction release by buyer, `formatMoney`, a
viewer query (handle, display name, role), drafts editable and private.
E2E selectors move to roles and test ids before the restyle touches
copy. Two plans: A, foundation and the existing screens; B, the new
pages (feed, landing, `/me`, `/h/{handle}`, `/host` grouping, draft
